# • **SKILLS AND TALENTS** •

*'Sure, playing Find the Empress is largely luck with only a modicum of skill. But try playing without that modicum, and you'll lose every time.'*

— Vulder Braudenmire, Gambler, Raconteur, and Liar

Can your Character jump the ravine or will they plunge to their death? Will they charm the officious courtier, or will the guards be summoned to deal with them? Your Character's chance of success is based on their Skills and Talents. Skills are learned and developed proficiencies. Talents are innate abilities, or tricks and insights you have gained through experience.

All Skills are associated with a single Characteristic, noted in the Skill's description and on the Character Sheet. A Skill's value = Characteristic + Skill Advances. Skills advance in +5 increments.

**Example:** *Sigrid has a* Fellowship *of 41 and has taken 2 Advances in* Charm*, so her* Charm *is 51 (41 + 10 = 51).*

### **SKILLS**

There are three types of Skill:

- **Basic Skills** can be used by anyone, even without any Advances.
- **Advanced Skills** can only be used if you have at least one Advance in the Skill.
- **Grouped Skills** are groups of related skills, each one covering a particular area of specialisation, eg. Lore (Empire) and Lore (Tilea).

### **Basic and Advanced Skills**

Basic Skills are those that anyone might practice in day-to-day life, without specialised training. Basic Skills can be Tested even if you have taken no Advances in them, using the Characteristic associated with the Skill.

**Example:** *Sigrid has no Advances in* Athletics*. However, as*  Athletics *is a Basic Skill, she can still Test* Athletics *using the Characteristic associated with it:* Agility*.*

Advanced Skills require specialist knowledge, training, or firsthand experience to even attempt to use. You may only Test an Advanced Skill if you have taken at least one Advance in it.

**Example:** *Adhemar has an* Intelligence *of 36 and no Advances in either the Basic* Gamble *Skill or the Advanced* Heal *Skill, both based on* Intelligence*. He can Test the* Gamble *Skill at 36 as it is Basic, and anyone can Test Basic Skills. The* Heal *Skill is an Advanced Skill, so Adhemar has no idea how to patch up wounds and can do nothing more useful to help an injured comrade than screaming loudly for a doctor!*

### **Grouped Skills and Specialisation**

Grouped Skills are an array of related Skills gathered together under one heading. Each related Skill is called a Specialisation. When a Specialisation is mentioned in the rules, it is marked in brackets.

*Example:* Language *is a Grouped Skill that represents many related Language Skills under a single Skill heading. Its Specialisations include* Language (Bretonnian)*,* Language (Magick)*, and*  Language (Mootish)*.* 

When you gain an Advance in a Grouped Skill, you must allocate the Advance to an appropriate Specialisation. Sometimes the Specialisation options will be marked clearly in your Career, so you simply select one of the options on offer. In other cases such as when a Specialisation is marked as 'Any One', meaning you can choose one Specialisation — you will need to select a Specialisation yourself.

**Example:** *Theodora has a choice of* Lore (Any One) *in her Career. When she allocates an Advance to that Skill, she has to decide which*  Lore *Specialisation to take. After some deliberation, she chooses to Advance her* Lore (Theology)*, showing she has improved her understanding of religious matters.*

Other than that, Grouped Skills are handled in exactly the same way as normal Skills, with each Specialisation handled like a unique Skill.

**Example:** *Sigrid has 1 Advance in the Basic Skill* Entertain (Singing)*, but nothing in* Entertain (Acting)*. This means she Tests her* Fellowship *of 41 when attempting to act, and Tests 46 when singing (41 + 5 = 46). By comparison, Theodora has a single Advance in the Advanced Skill* Animal Training (Pigeon)*, but no Advances in* Animal Training (Horse)*. This means she can use the Skill to train her pigeons to drop bombs on unsuspecting enemies, but she cannot train her horse to charge into a formation of enemy troops, as she has no idea how to train horses.*

### **Animal Care (Int)** *advanced*

Care for animals and heal them from disease or injury. A Character with an Advance in this Skill can keep animals healthy under normal conditions without needing to Test. A Test is required to diagnose or resolve problems with animals, such as spotting signs of illness or healing an injury (page 171).

### **Animal Training (Int)** *advanced, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Demigryph, Dog, Hawk, Horse, Pigeon

Your understanding of a particular type of animal, and ability to train them. To train a beast, undertake the *Animal Training*  Endeavour between adventures (see page 196).

### **Art (Dex)** *basic, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Drawing, Engraving, Mosaics, Painting, Sculpture, Tattooing, Weaving, Writing.

Create works of art in your chosen medium. The more SL achieved, the better the quality of the final piece. For complicated or large works of art, an Extended Test may be required.

### **Athletics (Ag)** *basic*

Your ability to run, jump, move or perform any other general physical activity.

### **Bribery (Fel)** *basic*

Find someone who is willing to accept a bribe, such as a guild notary to accept forged paperwork or a gate guard to look the other way while you slip inside after nightfall. You can also make a Bribery Test to judge if a particular person could be bribed, and what their price is likely to be.

### **Channelling (WP)** *advanced, grouped*

**Specialisations Include:** *Aqshy*, *Azyr*, *Chamon*, *Dhar*, *Ghur*, *Ghyran*, *Hysh*, *Magick*, *Shyish*, *Ulgu*.

Ability to call upon and control the various Winds of Magic, solely used with the magic rules. See **Chapter 8: Magic** for details.

### **Charm (Fel)** *basic*

Makes people think favourably of you, your opinions, and actions you propose. People won't necessarily do what you say simply because you've passed a Charm Test, but they will at least give you due consideration.

### **Charm Animal (WP)** *basic*

Your aptitude for befriending, quickly calming, or subjugating animals. A Charm Animal Test can convince a wild animal to back off, or a guard dog to devour a proffered scrap of meat rather than your arm.

IV **Climb (S)** *basic* The ability to ascend steep or vertical surfaces, like a perilous mountainside or the rain-slick wall of a tumbledown townhouse. Climbing can sometimes be used in combat when facing especially monstrous foes — see page 162 for combat movement.

### **Consume Alcohol (T)** *basic*

How well you can handle alcohol without letting it cloud your judgment or render you senseless. Often used to impress those who find the ability to down an unreasonable number of beverages to be praiseworthy.

### **Cool (WP)** *basic*

Allows you to remain calm under stress, resist fear in the face of horror, and stick to your convictions. It is generally used to resist the attempts of others to Charm or Intimidate you, or to curtail other Psychological effects (see page 183).

### **Dodge (Ag)** *basic*

Avoid things through ducking, diving, and moving quickly, and is used extensively to sidestep falling rocks, incoming weapons, and the like. In combat, Dodge is generally used to resist attacks or avoid damage.

### **Drive (Ag)** *basic*

Maneuvering vehicles — most commonly simple carts and lumbering coaches, but also the more experimental creations of Imperial Engineers — along the roads of the Empire with as little incident as possible.

### **Endurance (T)** *basic*

Called upon when you must endure hardship, withstand deprivation, stand without moving for long periods of time, or survive harsh environments. In particular, Endurance is Tested to resist or recover from various Conditions (see page 184) and helps you recover lost Wounds (see page 170).

### **Entertain (Fel)** *basic, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Acting, Comedy, Singing, Storytelling. Delight crowds with the spoken word, perhaps by singing, acting, or attempting a few jokes — hopefully entertaining all those within earshot.

### **Evaluate (Int)** *advanced*

Lets you determine the value of goods. Everybody is assumed to know the relative worth of general items, but Evaluate allows you to identify the value of curious and unique items, recognise forgeries, or spot flaws.

### **Gamble (Int)** *basic*

Measure the likelihood that a bet will pay off, as well as successfully engage in various games of chance. If you wish to improve your chances through cheating, see page 135.

### **Gossip (Fel)** *basic*

Ferret out interesting and useful news, and spread rumours of your own. Best practised at a town square, inn, well, or other gathering place.

### **Haggle (Fel) basic**

Secure better deals when negotiating with others, when purchasing goods for example. Typically involves an Opposed Haggle Test (page 131).

### **Heal (Int)** *advanced*

You have been trained to deal with injuries and diseases. A successful Heal Test allows you to diagnose illness, treat a disease, heal wounds or staunch a *Bleeding* Condition. Full rules for illness, injury, and treatments can be found on page 172.

### **Intimidate (S)** *basic*

Coerce or frighten sentient creatures — generally with an overt threat, though sometimes a subtle implication or even a look is enough.

### **Intuition (I)** *basic*

Gives you a feel for your surroundings, to notice when something is wrong, and read people to sense when they are lying or hiding something.

### **Language (Int)** *advanced, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Bretonnian, Reikspiel, Wastelander, and many more. See page 149.

Access to extra languages beyond your native tongue. A single Advance is enough to make yourself understood in that language, but a Test may be required to comprehend complicated discussions. Once you have 4 Advances, you count as fluent and no longer need to make Tests.

### **Leadership (Fel)** *basic*

Your ability to lead others and command respect. While most often associated with martial situations, a resolute leader can quickly direct a fast response to a fire or other such calamity, and nobles use the Skill frequently to command their lessers.

### **Lore (Int)** *advanced, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Engineering, Geology, Heraldry, History, Law, Local\*, Magic, Metallurgy, Science, Theology.

Knowledge of a subject you have been taught or have first-hand knowledge of. Basic facts related to a specific Lore Skill can be recalled easily, with a Test required only to recall obscure or unusual information.

### **Melee (WS)** *basic, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Basic, Brawling, Cavalry, Fencing, Flail, Parry, Polearm, Two-handed.

Training and experience with a single type of close combat weaponry. Each Melee Specialisation indicates expertise in using a specific class of weapon. If you do not have the correct Specialisation for a weapon, you will usually suffer a penalty when using it — see **Chapter 11: Consumer Guide** for more.

### **Navigation (I)** *basic*

Allows you to find your way in the wilderness, or across the ocean, using landmarks, stellar bodies, charts, or maps.

### **Outdoor Survival (Int)** *basic*

Used to survive in the wild, including the ability to fish, hunt, forage, and build fires and shelters. Experienced survivors are practiced at reading the signs of incoming inclement weather, making do with minimal tools, and noticing the spoor of local beasts.

### **Perception (I)** *basic*

Your ability to notice things with your senses — sight, smell, hearing, touch, taste, and any other senses you may possess, such as magical or inhuman senses.

### **Perform (Ag)** *advanced, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Acrobatics, Clowning, Dancing, Firebreathing, Juggling, Miming, Rope Walking.

You have learned a physically demanding art, perhaps as a way to make a living. A successful Perform Test lets you entertain those near enough to see you.

### **Pick Lock (Dex)** *advanced*

You know the mechanisms of locks and how to open them without their proper keys. Many locks are simple affairs made to vex only untutored thieves, while others can be complex dwarf-made mechanisms capable of frustrating all but the most determined plunderer.

### **Play (Dex)** *advanced, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Bagpipe, Lute, Harpsichord, Horn, Violin. Make music with an instrument, hopefully well enough to impress others. A successful Play Test lets you entertain those near enough to hear you.

### **THE OLD WORLD'S A STAGE…**

While there is some overlap between the Skills of Entertain, Perform, and Play, each reflects a distinct group of Skills.

**Entertain** is a Basic Skill using Fellowship. It represents those Skills of entertainment that anyone can attempt, even without training, such as singing or telling stories.

**Perform** is an Advanced Skill using Agility. Perform Specialisations require training to attempt, and rely on physical speed and coordination.

**Play** is also an Advanced Skill, this time using Dexterity. It represents training in specific musical instruments, all of which need teaching and practice to perform well.

You should choose carefully when determining whether your chosen Specialisation belongs to Entertain, Perform, or Play; consider how the Character will physically enact the Skill, and whether an untrained beginner could do so with any success. After all, someone without any musical training could feasibly sing beautifully, but hand them a set of bagpipes…

IV **Pray (Fel)** *advanced* Invoke, appeal to, or otherwise commune with a deity. For more information on using the Pray Skill to seek divine intervention, see **Chapter 7: Religion and Belief**.

### **Ranged (BS)** *advanced, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Blackpowder, Bow, Crossbow, Engineering, Entangling, Explosives, Sling, Throwing.

Anyone can throw a rock, but it takes training and practice to use weapons like bows, pistols, and throwing knives. Each Ranged Specialisation is specific to a group of ranged weapons. If you do not have the Ranged Specialisation for a weapon you wish to use, refer to **Chapter 11: Consumer Guide** for the penalties you will suffer when using the weapon.

### **Research (Int)** *advanced*

Finding useful and frequently obscure knowledge in libraries and other such storehouses of information. Research requires you to have the *Read/Write* Talent (see page 124). More on what kinds of information you can find and where to start looking can be found on page 150.

### **Ride (Ag)** *basic, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Demigryph, Griffon, Horse, Pegasus. Proficiency at riding a particular group of animals; Ride (Horse), for example, will let you ride horses, mules, donkeys, and similar. See page 168 for rules on fighting from the back of a mount.

### **Row (S)** *basic*

Your prowess at pulling an oar to move a boat through the water. A Row Test is typically only needed when racing, navigating rapids, desperately avoiding bog octopuses, or similar challenging feats.

### **Sail (Ag)** *advanced*

Operate and manoeuvre a sailing vessel — including knotwork, steering, gauging the wind, and more. Sail is required to operate your own vessel, either alone or as part of a crew. If you are commanding a well trained crew, use Leadership instead.

### **LOCAL LORES**

When you take the Lore (Local) skill, choose a locality that your Character is familiar with, eg. The Reikland, Tilea, Ubersreik, or The Street of a Thousand Taverns. If you take this Skill at character creation, decide with the GM what this should be — it could be your home village, or another place you are familiar with.

### **Secret Signs (Int)** *advanced, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Grey Order, Guild (Any One), Ranger, Scout, Thief, Vagabond.

You have been taught how to use clandestine markings only intelligible to members of a select group. Vagabonds might indicate which homeowners are likely to offer alms, thieves may want to indicate territorial markings or warnings, while scouts may want to warn one another about a dangerous monster's lair nearby (see page 138).

### **Set Trap (Dex)** *advanced*

From a simple snare or bear trap, to more spectacular devices like Von Grizzel's Thief-render, players are unlikely to venture far in the Old World without encountering traps. The Set Trap Skill measures your ability to set and disarm traps of all varieties.

### **Sleight of Hand (Dex)** *advanced*

Your ability to pick pockets, palm objects, and perform minor tricks of prestidigitation, as well as cheat at games of chance. This Skill is typically Opposed by the Perception Skill of your target.

### **Stealth (Ag)** *basic, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Rural, Underground, Urban.

Move and hide without being detected, blend into a crowd, or otherwise avoid drawing attention to yourself.

### **Swim (S)** *advanced*

Moving and staying afloat in water. If you have the Swim Skill, you are automatically presumed to be able to swim freely without a Test. But if you find yourself in difficult currents, racing, or fleeing from a ravenous stirpike, a Test will be required.

### **Track (I)** *advanced*

Your ability to follow subtle trails left by others. Track is used to follow and find your target, as well as to hide your trail.

### **Trade (Dex)** *advanced, grouped*

**Specialisations:** Apothecary, Calligrapher, Chandler, Carpenter, Cook, Embalmer, Engineer, Herbalist, Shipwright, Smith, Tanner.

Your ability to practice a trade, to create something or provide a service, as well as your knowledge of the relevant lore surrounding your trade.

## **TALENTS**

You choose your initial Talents as part of creating your Character. Once your adventures have begun, you can buy additional Talents using Experience Points (XP) between adventures (see page 191), usually those from your current Career Level and the levels below it. Some Talents can be taken multiple times, as explained in their descriptions.

### **Accurate Shot**

You are an exceptional shot and know where to shoot an enemy to inflict maximum damage. Your successful Ranged Attacks inflict +1 Damage, or +2 if you took the time to aim (page 167).

### **Acute Sense (Sense)**

One of your primary five senses is highly developed, allowing you to spot what others miss. You may take Perception Tests to detect normally imperceptible details with the associated sense, as dictated by the GM. For example, you might be able to hear the difference between the cry of one wolf and another, or taste the difference between the exact same vintage of wine bottled on two different days. You may take this Talent multiple times, choosing a new sense each time you do so.

### **Aethyric Attunement**

Your experience, talent, or training lets you accumulate magical energy with a reduced risk of miscasts. If you would roll on the **Minor Miscast Table**, you may roll twice and choose whichever result you prefer. You may purchase this Talent a second time to gain the same benefit when rolling on the **Major Miscast Table**.

### **Alley Cat**

You are at home in cities, able to move about without drawing attention to yourself and vanish into any crowd. You gain Advantage on Stealth (Urban) Tests, and can figure out the quickest or least noticeable way to get across any town or city you are familiar with without making a Test.

### **Ambidextrous**

You can use your off-hand far better than most folk, either by training or innate talent. You do not suffer the –2 SL penalty to Tests relying solely on your secondary hand, including attacks made with weapons held in that hand.

### **Animal Affinity**

Wild animals feel comfortable in your presence, treating you much as they would an otherwise innocuous part of the environment. As long as you do not act aggressively towards them, wild animals will not ordinarily attack you.

### **Arcane Magic (Lore)**

You either study one of the eight Colour Lores of Magic (Beasts, Death, Fire, Heavens, Life, Light, Metal, or Shadows), a Witch Lore such as Hedgecraft or Witchcraft, or a Dark Lore like Daemonology or Necromancy. Upon learning the Talent, you gain one Spell of your choice from your chosen Lore. You may now memorise additional Spells from your chosen Lore for the following cost in XP.

You may normally learn only one Arcane Lore of Magic. Dark Lores are an exception: depending on your path of study (and with the GM's permission), you may learn a single Dark Lore in addition to one of the other Arcane Lores. You can unlearn this Talent at a cost of 100 XP, but you will immediately lose all Spells linked to the Talent if you do so.

### **Argumentative**

You are especially skilled at drawing others into a quagmire of prolonged and frequently pointless argument. When you argue with someone, they must pass a Cool Test, opposed by your Charm, to disengage from a pointless debate with you. While arguing they are distracted, suffering a -3 SL penalty to most Tests unrelated to you, unless a more important issue is brought to their attention, or they are attacked.

### **Artistic (Art)**

You have a natural talent for art. You may always advance the Art Skill, even if it is not part of your current Career. When you choose this Talent, pick one form of written or representative art, such as painting or poetry. You gain Advantage on Art Tests when working in that medium. You may purchase this Talent more than once, choosing another art form in which to excel.

### **Attractive**

Whether it is your piercing eyes or the flash of your perfect teeth, you know how to make the best use of what the gods gave you. When you successfully use Charm to influence those attracted to you, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die.

IV **Battle Rage** You are always on the edge of self-control, ready to slip into a violent *Frenzy* at any moment (see page 184). If another ability would allow you to enter a state of *Frenzy*, you may do so without making a Test. Furthermore, when you exit the *Frenzy*  state, you do not incur a *Fatigued* Condition.

### **Beat Blade**

You are trained to make sharp, controlled blows to your opponent's weapon, creating an opening for an attack. Upon a successful Melee Attack, you may choose to forgo inflicting any Damage. If you do so, your opponent may not Oppose further Melee Tests with any Melee Skill until the end of their next Turn, and Melee Attacks made against them gain the benefit of Advantage. They may continue to oppose attacks with the Dodge Skill. This Talent is of no use if your opponent has a larger Size than you (see page 360).

### **Beneath Notice**

You are adept at blending into situations that involve your social betters, fading into the background by showing deference to those who think themselves above you. As long as you are properly attired and make some effort to fit in, most people will simply assume you are meant to be there. Gate guards and the like may ask your business, but any plausible explanation is sufficient for them to grant you passage. This Talent affords no protection if you do something to draw attention to yourself such as fumbling a plate full of drinking cups or brandishing a weapon.

### **Berserk Charge**

You hurl yourself at your enemies with reckless abandon, using the force of your charge to add weight to your strike. When you Charge, add +2 Damage to your strike.

### **Blather**

You are adept at offering effortless, entirely false justifications for being somewhere you shouldn't be, or doing something you shouldn't be doing. When you are caught, make a Charm Test, Opposed by your target's Cool. If you pass, your preposterous explanation convinces them for one Turn (or minute, depending on the situation) per SL you achieved on the Opposed Test. If you achieved a number of SL greater than your target's Willpower Bonus, they remain convinced until someone else points out the incongruity in your explanation.

### **Bless (Deity)**

You are watched over by one of the gods and can empower simple prayers. Upon learning the Talent, you manifest all the Blessings of the deity you worship.

Under normal circumstances, you may not learn more than one *Bless (Deity)* Talent. Further, you may not learn the Channelling Skill or the *Arcane Magic*, *Chaos Magic*, *Magic Resistance*, *Petty Magic*, or *Witch!* Talents when you have the *Bless* Talent, and vice versa. You can unlearn this Talent for 100 XP, but will lose all of your Blessings if you do so, and may garner the disfavour of the god you have spurned, with effects determined by your GM.

### **Bookish**

You are an avid reader, and know how to quickly navigate your way around a library. You gain Advantage on any Research Test you make.

### **Break and Enter**

Just by observing a building, locked room, or vault, you can determine what looks to be the fastest and quietest route in. Additionally, you have Advantage on Pick Lock Tests, or Strength Tests made to force open doors or windows.

### **Briber**

You need only share a few words with someone to know if they are open to a bribe, and exactly how much would be required to have them turn a blind eye, or even assist in some nefarious activity. You have Advantage on any Bribery Test you make.

### **Cardsharp**

You are used to playing, and winning, at games, although your methods *may* involve a little cheating. When making an Opposed Gamble Test, if you roll a double, you may reroll your opponent's Test. This additionally negates any benefit your opponent may have gained with Sleight of Hand.

### **Careful Strike**

You are skilled at hitting your enemy exactly where you want to. When making a Melee Attack, the penalty for making a Called Shot is reduced to -1 SL. If your attack is made with Advantage, you may make a Called Shot at no penalty.

#### **Carouser**

You are a seasoned drinker and know how to party hard. You do not suffer any ill effects from consuming modest amounts of alcohol, and only need to make a Consume Alcohol Test if drinking to excess — for example, if attempting to impress a bar full of dwarfs. You gain Advantage on any Consume Alcohol Test you make, as well as any Endurance Test you make to resist the effects of poison.

### **Catfall**

You are as nimble and balanced as a cat. Whenever you fall, it counts as a deliberate fall, allowing you to make an Athletics Test to reduce any Damage incurred. You also gain Advantage on Athletics Tests made while falling.

### **Cat-tongued**

Like Ranald the Trickster God, you blend truth and lies as if there were no difference. You gain Advantage on any Charm Test you make to lie convincingly.

### **Chaos Magic (Lore)**

By accident or design, you have lost a portion of your soul to one of the Dark Gods, and can now practise the foul magics of Chaos. Your ruinous patron immediately grants you access to Spells from the chosen Chaos Lore. Upon learning the Talent, you gain one Spell of your choice from your chosen Lore. You may now memorise additional Spells from your chosen Lore for the following cost in XP.

Each time you obtain a Spell of your chosen Lore, you gain a Corruption Point as the spell infiltrates your mind, never to be forgotten.

Under normal circumstances, you may not learn more than one Lore of Chaos. Unlike the *Arcane Lore* Talent, once learned, this Talent may never be unlearned.

### **Combat Aware**

You are used to scanning the battlefield to make snap decisions informed by the shifting tides of war. You may take a **Challenging (+0 SL) Perception** Test to avoid gaining the *Surprised* Condition. If the GM would normally allow a Test to ignore Surprise in this circumstance, you automatically succeed.

### **Combat Master**

You are adept at making your presence felt on the battlefield, using your size or speed to corner your opponents. You count as two people for the purposes of outnumbering an opponent, or when determining if your side is outnumbered. See page 167 for the rules for outnumbering.

You react quickly to danger, leaping to attack your enemy without pause. When you roll for Combat Initiative, roll twice and choose your prefered result.

### **Commanding Presence**

Your presence inspires awe and admiration. In the absence of recognised authority, most people naturally look to you for leadership in a crisis and follow any reasonable orders you give. In addition, you gain Advantage on Leadership Tests.

### **Concoct**

You are skilled at making potions and draughts on the go. You may make a free *Crafting* Endeavour each session to use Trade (Apothecary, Herbalist, or Poisoner) without the need for a Workshop. Trade Tools and raw materials are still required. Due to the rushed nature of the creation, it expires at the start of the next Downtime, regardless of whether the item has been completed or not.

### **Contortionist**

You can bend and manipulate your body in a myriad of seemingly unnatural ways. This allows you to squeeze through gaps that would normally be difficult or impossible, at the GM's discretion. You gain Advantage on any Athletics Test made to escape from bonds.

### **Coolheaded**

If you wish, you can even adopt this persona for the purpose of carrying out *Banking* and *Income* Endeavours, however, in the case of an *Income* Endeavour, you must take a **Challenging (+0 SL) Earning** Test with either Entertain (Acting) or the chosen Career's normal Earning Skill for the Earning Test (not the Earning Skill of your actual Career). If you have the *Embezzle* Talent, you can use Bribery or Evaluate instead if you are embezzling. If the Earning Test fails, the ruse is exposed, with consequences determined by the GM.

#### **Shadow**

You are skilled at following people through busy crowds and winding streets without losing your mark. You instinctively know when to abandon a tail before your quarry spots you. If someone you are following succeeds on a Perception Test to spot you, you may either allow them to spot you and continue following them, or lose track of them and remain unseen.

#### **Sharp**

Gain +5 Initiative. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Sharpshooter**

You can make shots of exceptional accuracy. Ranged Tests you make do not incur any penalties from your target being *Prone* or in cover. You may also perform feats of accuracy with your weapons that most would think impossible, such as cutting a hangman's noose with an arrow or punching a hole in a tossed coin with a pistol shot. Such feats of marksmanship still require a successful Ranged Test, with the GM setting the difficulty.

#### **Shieldsman**

You are skilled at using your shield to control the flow of battle. Once per Round, while carrying a shield, you may lose Momentum to either grant your shield's AP bonus to an adjacent ally who is not carrying a shield, or, if you win an Opposed Melee Test while attacking or defending, push your opponent up to 2 yards directly away and no longer count as Engaged.

#### **Sixth Sense**

You get a strange feeling whenever danger is near. If you are about to trigger a trap or fall victim to a hidden danger, such as an ambush, collapsing tunnel, or poisoned glass of wine, you may make a **Challenging (+0 SL) Intuition** Test. On a success, you detect the danger at the last possible moment.

#### **Slayer**

You are undaunted by large enemies and attack them with great gusto. If you Charge a creature whose Size is larger than you, you automatically gain Momentum.

#### **Small**

You are much shorter than most folk in the Old World. Gain *Size (Small)*. The full rules for different Sizes are found in **Chapter 12: Bestiary** on page 360.

#### **Sniper**

Distance is of no import to your shooting skills, and you are just as adept at picking off far away targets as those nearby. You suffer no penalties for shooting at Long Range, and only a -1 SL penalty for shots made at Extreme Range.

#### **Speedreader**

You read books at a voracious pace. A single glance is all you require to read a full page of text, and you can easily make your way through even a lengthy tome in a single day. When you make an Extended Research Test, double the SL added by any successful Test you make.

#### **Sprinter**

You are a swift runner with a good level of physical fitness. You gain Advantage on Athletics Tests to run and jump.

#### **Step Aside**

You are skilled at being where enemy weapons are not. If you successfully use Dodge to defend against an attack, you may move up to 2 yards as you dive away, and no longer count as Engaged. Opponents do not gain a free attack when you do this.

#### **Stone Soup**

You are used to getting by with less, and know how to survive lean times. If a member of the party makes a Foraging Test (see page 152), they automatically locate sufficient food to feed you, over and above the number of Characters fed by the Test result, even if the Test fails.

#### **Stout-hearted**

No matter how bad things get, you always seem to come back for more. The first time in an encounter you gain one or more *Broken* Conditions, you gain one less.

#### **Striding Gait (Terrain)**

You are experienced in traversing difficult ground. You ignore all movement penalties when crossing over or through a specified terrain. If engaged in a Pursuit in your chosen terrain, you receive +2 SL to all Tests relating to the Pursuit. Example Terrains are: Coastal, Desert, Rocky, Tundra, Wetland, Woodland. You may take this Talent more than once, choosing a new Terrain each time you do.

#### **Strike Mighty Blow**

You know how to hit hard! You inflict +1 extra Damage with melee weapons, or +2 if your attack was made with Advantage.

#### **Strike to Injure**

You are an expert at striking your enemies' most vulnerable areas. When rolling on a Critical Wound table, roll twice and choose which result you prefer. Further, you ignore the Called Shot penalty when going for a Silent Takedown (see page 135).

#### **Strike to Stun**

You know how to use your weapon to subdue, not kill. You count all Improvised Weapons as having the *Pummel* Quality. When using a *Pummel* weapon, ignore the Called Shot penalty when going for a Silent Takedown (see page 135).

#### **Strong Back**

You have a strong back that is used to hard work. You gain a bonus of +1 SL to any Opposed Strength Test, and the amount of Encumbrance you can carry increases by one. If you wish, you may purchase this Talent a second time, increasing the bonus to +3 SL and the number of additional Encumbrance points to three.

#### **Strong Legs**

You have strong legs able to carry you great distances when you jump. When you successfully use the Athletics Skill to sprint or leap, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die.

#### **Strong-minded**

You are the epitome of determination and resolve. Once per session, before taking a Test, you may choose to take that Test without any negative modifiers.

#### **Strong Swimmer**

You are an accomplished swimmer, able to fight a strong current and outpace other swimmers. You gain Advantage on Swim Tests, and receive a +2 SL Bonus to swimming Pursuits.

IV **Sturdy** You have a brawny physique, or are very used to carrying things. When calculating the number of Encumbrance Points you can carry, count your Strength Bonus twice.

#### **Suave**

Gain +5 Fellowship. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Super Numerate**

You have a gift for calculation and can work out solutions to most mathematical problems with ease, without the need for a quill, paper, or abacus. You gain Advantage on Evaluate Tests, and you can keep track of the passage of time accurately so long as you are conscious.

#### **Supportive**

You know what to say and when to make the most impact upon your superiors. You not only ignore the penalty to using Charm on those of a higher Status Tier, but also gain Advantage on Charm Tests made to ingratiate yourself with those of higher Status.

#### **Sure Shot**

You are skilled in placing your shots exactly where they will do the most damage, tearing through vital organs and finding vulnerabilities in your target's armour. Your Ranged Attacks inflict +1 Damage, and may ignore armour with the *Partial* Flaw. If your weapon has the *Impale* Quality, you can ignore armour with the *Weakpoints* Flaw as well.

#### **Surgery**

You are a surgeon, able to open and close the flesh in order to heal others. When you successfully use the Heal Skill outside of combat, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die. Furthermore, you can treat any Critical Wound marked as needing Surgery, for effects see pages 172 to 179.

#### **Tenacious**

You are a vigorous sort. The first time in an encounter you would gain one or more *Fatigued* Conditions, you gain one less.

#### **Tinker**

You are somewhat of a Johann-of-all-trades, able to repair almost anything. You count all Trade Skills as Basic when repairing broken items. If you have at least 1 Advance in a Trade Skill, you have Advantage on Tests to repair associated items, and such repairs take half as much time.

#### **Tower of Memories**

A recollection technique first instigated by the Cult of Verena, reputedly from elven practices taught by the loremasters of Hoeth, *Tower of Memories* allows you to perfectly recall a significant amount of facts by storing them in an imaginary spire. When you successfully use the Lore Skill, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die.

#### **Trapper**

You are skilled at using all manner of traps, and know how to make basic snares and pitfalls from whatever comes to hand. You gain Advantage on Set Trap Tests. Additionally, with an hour to prepare, you can craft a small animal trap or covered pit (see page 136).

#### **Trick Rider**

You are capable of amazing feats of agility while in the saddle. You can use any of your Perform Skills and unmodified Dodge Skill when mounted.

#### **Tunnel Fighter**

You are at home in tunnels, sewers, and other underground environments. You gain Advantage on Stealth (Underground) Tests, and can easily tell how far you have travelled underground and roughly how deep beneath the surface you are.

#### **Unshakeable**

You are a jaded veteran who has survived more than one hail of shots from blackpowder weapons. You do not need to Test to resist a *Broken* Condition if shot at by a *Blackpowder* weapon.

#### **Very Resilient**

Gain +5 Toughness. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Very Strong**

Gain +5 Strength. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **War Leader**

Your stern gaze and inspiring words motivate your soldiers to fight on to the end. All allies you influence with Leadership during battle (see page 169) may use your Leadership rather than their Cool to resist *Fear* until the end of the battle. The effect ends if you die, or while you are subject to *Broken*, *Unconscious*, or *Fear* Conditions.

#### **Warrior Born**

Gain +5 Weapon Skill. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **War Wizard**

You are trained to cast magic while in the thick of combat. On your Turn, you may cast one Spell with a Casting Number of 5 or less for free without using your Action. If you do this, you may not cast another Spell this Turn.

#### **Waterman**

You are an experienced freshwater sailor and are well-versed with river vessels. When you successfully use the Row or Sail Skill on a river or lake, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die. You count as two crewmen towards the minimum number of crew to pilot a river vessel.

#### **Wealthy**

You are fabulously wealthy and are rarely ever short of coin. For the purpose of securing money through your Career, either during play or performing an *Income* Endeavour, you obtain 1 additional gold crown. You may take this Talent a second time, allowing you to secure 3 additional gold crowns instead.

#### **Well-prepared**

You are used to anticipating the needs of others, and yourself. Once per session, you may pull a Trapping required for the current situation from your pockets, as long as it is Encumbrance 0, could feasibly have been bought recently, and does not stretch credulity too far. You can use this Talent more times in the same session by spending Fortune. Whenever you use this ability, you must deduct the cost for the item, representing money you spent earlier. If you have no money, you may not use this Talent.

#### **Witch!**

You have learned magic through trial and error. Gain one Spell of your choice from any Colour Lore or the Lore of Witchcraft, even if you lack the appropriate *Arcane Magic* Talent. Further, treat that Spell's Channelling Skill as a Basic Skill. You can memorise an additional such Spell (and treat its Channelling Skill as Basic) for 150 XP; further Spells cost 50 XP more each time.

Possession of this Talent may attract the attention of witch hunters, and complicate matters should you wish to join an established magical tradition. You can spend 100 XP to lose this Talent and all associated Spells.
